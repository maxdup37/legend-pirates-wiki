Aggresive Tentacle

The Aggresive Tentacle is a special monster that deals area-of-effect (AoE) damage to players and is closely linked to Kraken encounters.

Map Locations

  • From Kraken & Abyssal Kraken: Level 13–14 maps and PvE: Kraken Pit.
  • From Tentacle: Level 10–14 maps after killing 50 Tentacles per map (each map has its own counter).

Stats & Rewards

  • HP: 70,000 – 75,000
  • AoE Damage per tile: 5,000 – 6,000
  • Rewards:
    • x1 Kraken Soul
    • x1 Tentacle Meats (dropped on sea)
    • Abyssal Kraken and PvE Dragon Pit Kraken Aggressive Tentacles can drop the same Tentacle Meats.

Spawn Rules with Kraken HP

When Abyssal Kraken and Kraken HP drop to certain thresholds:

  • At 80% HP → 10x Aggresive Tentacle spawn
  • At 60% HP → 10x Aggresive Tentacle spawn
  • At 40% HP → 10x Aggresive Tentacle spawn
  • At 20% HP → 10x Aggresive Tentacle spawn

Tentacle

Tentacles are special monsters that appear in higher-level maps and are directly tied to the spawning of Aggresive Tentacles.

Map Locations

  • Level 10–14 maps.

Stats & Rewards

  • HP: 15,000 – 17,500
  • Attack: 1,750 – 2,000
  • Reward: x1 Kraken Soul

Spawn Interaction with Aggresive Tentacle

  • When you kill 50 Tentacles on a given map (10–14), 1 Aggresive Tentacle will spawn:
    • On the same map
    • At the position of the last Tentacle killed
  • Each map (10–14) has its own independent kill counter for spawning Aggresive Tentacles.
