Key

This item is used to unlock different types of chests found on the sea map.

  • This item will stay in your inventory.
  • Available at Tharun NPC with crafting method.
  • Weekly craftable limit: 10,000 Keys.
  • Always craftable.
  • The crafting requirement increases gradually after specific thresholds.

Regular Chest

Unlockable chest found on sea maps using 1 Key. Contains many useful rewards: Frost Ball, Fire Cannonball, Season Cannonball, Cannon Oil, Mariners Hearthstone, Aether Barrier, Sailor Salvation, Repair Wood, Diamond, Fortune Crystal, Gold.

  • Each map contains: 20 Regular Chests
  • Respawn system like Flotsam
  • Unlock Condition: Move above the chest with at least 1 Key
