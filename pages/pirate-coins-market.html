Pirate Coins Market

The Pirate Coins Market is a special section of the Shop where players can spend Pirate Coins and Diamonds to purchase powerful buffs.

Pirate Coins are earned by completing Daily Tasks, then used to unlock permanent or time-limited advantages in battle.

Location: In-game Shop → Pirate Coins Points Market.

🪙 How Pirate Coins Work

  • Pirate Coins Points Market is located in the Shop.
  • Pirate Coins are obtained when a player completes their Daily Task.
  • Pirate Coins can then be used to buy special buffs.
  • Daily Task coins are not removed – a new coin (Pirate Coin) is added, used only for this market.
Important Note: Daily Task coin will not be removed. A new coin, Pirate Coin, is added and will be used specifically for the Pirate Coins Market to unlock buffs.

📜 Buff List

  • Heart Of Colossus Buff – 20,000 more HP
  • Windrunner Sigil Buff – 5% Speed
  • Rivalbreaker Totem Buff – 10% Damage on Players
  • Ironveil Talisman Buff – 5% Protection Damage
