Elite Point Paper (EP Paper)

EP Paper is a special item that can be earned as a reward from NPCs located on the game map within your current elite class. It is obtained under specific conditions, depending on your active elite class and the map you are on.

To check the conditions under which these items can be obtained, please refer to the dedicated tables and guides for each elite class and map.

⚙️ Features

  • Capacity: Players can hold up to 100 EP Paper items.
  • Location in HUD: EP Paper is listed under the booster category.
  • Usage: Clicking the item opens a pop-up menu with Use x1 and Use All options.
  • Usage Restriction: After reaching Elite Class Level 24, you can no longer drop EP Paper items.

📍 Availability & Restrictions

EP Paper can be obtained as a reward from NPCs on maps that match your current elite class, as long as the specific drop conditions for that class and map are met.

Source EP Paper Availability
NPCs matching your elite class & map ✅ Can drop EP Paper if conditions are met
Tentacle Monster ❌ No EP Paper drop
Blizzan Monster ❌ No EP Paper drop
Elite Voyager NPCs ❌ No EP Paper drop

EP Paper item is not available from Tentacle Monster, Blizzan Monster and Elite Voyager NPCs.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Try to avoid hitting the capacity limit of 100 to not waste potential drops.
  • Use All when you want to push several elite levels quickly.
  • Plan your EP Paper usage around major milestones in your elite class progression.
  • Remember that after elite level 24, you cannot drop new EP Paper items anymore.
