Daily Ixir Reward Quests

You can interact with the Witch character in Safe Harbor to accept the quest, complete the required tasks, and then deliver them back to her.

You can track this quest from your Quest List or through the Skill button in the menu at the top of the screen.

Rewards

  • x1 Ixir
  • x10,000 Diamonds
  • x5,000 Frost Ball
  • x5,000 Eros Ball

Notes

  • You must visit the Witch to accept and submit the quest.
  • Once you accept the quest, a 24-hour cooldown begins for the next Ixir Reward Quest.
  • Even if you complete the quest later, your waiting time does not reset.
  • A total of 90 Ixir Reward Quests can be completed.

Ixir Brewing System & Skill Point Requirements

You can bring the Ixirs you collect to the Witch in Safe Harbor to generate Skill Points. Additional resources are also required for this process.

  • Once you deliver the required items, a brew time will begin.
  • This brew time increases as the Skill Point level increases.
  • You must visit the Witch to start the brewing and to claim the Skill Points after completion.
  • You can track the brewing progress via the Skill button in the top menu.
  • You can brew a maximum of 30 Skill Points.

Elite Voyager Quests – Ixir Reward

  • Each Elite Voyager quest now rewards 1 Ixir.
  • If you have already completed some or all of these quests, the rewards will be automatically added to your account based on your progress.

As soon as the update is live, you can use your Ixirs for Skill Point brewing.

Additional Skill Tree Information

  • A total of 30 Skill Points can be reached. To unlock all of them, players must have 122 Ixir and bring them to the Witch while also meeting all other requirements and brewing times.
  • With 90 Ixir Reward Quests and 10 Elite Voyager quests, obtaining 122 Ixir is very doable, even if it takes time.
  • To unlock "Fist of Agnis", "Wave Gunner" and "Battle Master", you must use at least 1 point in each required skill.
  • A one-time package containing 15 Ixir will be available in the Legend Pirates Store to help players progress faster. Extra Ixirs can be used in future updates when new skills are added.
  • All skills in the Skill Tree can be adjusted, improved, or changed based on players' in-game performance.
  • Before the Skill Tree update, all Ixir items obtained from Admiral NPCs will be removed from all accounts to ensure equal conditions for all players.

How to Use and Reset Skill Points

Using Skill Points

  • Click the Skill button at the top of the screen.
  • Allocate your Skill Points directly in the menu.
  • No need to switch to a special map – you can even adjust skills during battle.

Resetting Skill Points

  • Open the Skill menu.
  • Click the "Reset Skill" button under the Basic Skill section.
  • No need to stay in a special map.

Reset Cost

Each 1 Skill Point costs 4,000 Diamonds to reset.

  • Resetting 1 Skill Point = 4,000 Diamonds
  • Resetting 10 Skill Points = 40,000 Diamonds
  • Resetting 25 Skill Points = 100,000 Diamonds
