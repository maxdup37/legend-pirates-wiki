Daily Limit Gameplay

To encourage fair gameplay for all players, the previous debuff system has been replaced with a new, clearer system based on the number of hours played per day.

When a player exceeds a certain number of hours online, a progressive debuff is applied to reduce their effectiveness and promote healthier playtime.

Monday to Friday (12 Hours Limit)

From Monday to Friday, each player can play up to 12 hours per day without penalty.

If a player continues playing beyond 12 hours, a debuff starts to apply from the 13th hour and increases with each extra hour:

Hours Played (Day) Debuff
Up to 12 hours No debuff
13 hours -20%
14 hours -40%
15 hours -60%
16 hours -70%
17+ hours -80%

Saturday to Sunday (14 Hours Limit)

During the weekend (Saturday & Sunday), the time limit is slightly higher: players can play up to 14 hours without penalty.

If a player continues playing beyond 14 hours, the debuff applies starting from the 15th hour:

Hours Played (Day) Debuff
Up to 14 hours No debuff
15 hours -20%
16 hours -40%
17 hours -60%
18 hours -70%
19+ hours -80%

Summary & Fair Play

  • The system encourages healthy playtime and fair competition.
  • Debuffs are applied based on the total time played in a single day.
  • Weekdays: safe up to 12h, then heavy debuffs.
  • Weekends: safe up to 14h, then similar debuffs.
