Air Drop Global Event Tutorial

The Air Drop is a dynamic global event that brings players together on the same maps, offering rewards, PvP opportunities, and a cinematic multiphase animation sequence.

Air Drop Global Event Details 🆕

Phase 1 – Air Drop Incoming

  • 3 minutes before the Air Drop lands, a global notification appears for all players.
  • Example: "Air Drop spotted on map 8-2"
  • A flying parachute animation appears above the targeted map.
  • The animation scales up gradually to simulate descent.
  • The parachute’s shadow grows and stays aligned with the landing point.

Phase 2 – Floating Chest

  • Once it lands, a closed floating chest animation lasts for 3 minutes.

Phase 3 – Chest Opens

  • The chest opens and releases multiple Air Drop flotsams around the area.
  • Players race to collect the flotsams before they disappear.
  • The open-chest animation remains for up to 1 minute.

Benefits

  • 🎯 Extra PvP interaction on targeted maps.
  • 🔔 Global notifications attract players from the whole server.
  • 🌟 Showcases high-quality animated global events.
  • 💎 Extra reward opportunities.
  • 🤝 Encourages cooperative fights and group strategy.

Rules

  • Air Drops spawn randomly every 2 to 2.5 hours.
  • 2 or 3 different maps receive an Air Drop at the same time.
